About the center

Local, licensed, and centered on early discovery.

I Love Children Learning Center is a licensed child care center at 316 Claremont Avenue in Montclair, New Jersey. The current state record lists a licensed capacity of 19 children and an age range of 2½–6 years.

The public program description centers nature-based learning, hands-on experiences, creativity, outdoor discovery and early development. A fuller founder or team story will be added only after the owner confirms the details.
