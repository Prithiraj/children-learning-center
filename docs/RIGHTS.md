# Image provenance and rights notes

## Current repository state

The site intentionally ships **no third-party business photography**. All files under `assets/demo/` are original development placeholders and are visibly labelled in the UI as owner-photo launch blockers.

## Production photography requirements

Production imagery should be owner-controlled or otherwise clearly licensed for commercial website use. For identifiable children, confirm appropriate parent/guardian model releases. Confirm photographer rights separately when the business did not create the photograph itself.

## Sources used for factual research

Public sources such as the New Jersey DCF license register and the business-provided Winnie profile were used to verify text facts. Their photographs were not copied into this repository.

## Prohibited substitutions

Do not replace demo assets with:

- competitor photos;
- Google Maps review photos without explicit commercial-use permission;
- photos scraped from Winnie or directories without explicit commercial-use permission;
- generic stock images that imply they depict the center when they do not.

## Launch blocker

Replacing demo gallery/hero imagery with rights-cleared business photography is a production launch requirement.
